# Inspection Audit Skill

## Purpose

Structure inspection evidence without exceeding what the available materials support.

## Workflow

1. Identify object and inspected scope.
2. Identify accessible/non-accessible zones.
3. Extract observable facts.
4. Link each material fact to source evidence.
5. Classify data as PROJECT, ACTUAL, MEASURED, TESTED, CALCULATED, ASSUMED, INTERPRETED, or UNKNOWN.
6. Separate defect observation from presumed mechanism/cause.
7. Emit UNCERTAINTY when evidence is insufficient.

## Hard rules

- Do not invent measurements.
- Do not convert “not visible” into “absent”.
- Do not infer emergency condition from a single symptom.
- Do not alter a technically correct report merely to create findings.

## Output

Structured inspection findings with evidence references and status.
